## P1 BLOCKER — PT Data Buy/Sell History fails

**Reproduced by user ZIPs; unresolved.** The date-range exporter uses `page=0` upward, `perPage=25`, `sort=BOUGHTDATE` / `SOLDDATE`, `sortDirection=DESCENDING`, expected response envelope `{hashcode,data:[...]}` and schema-1.1 manifests. Each history dataset fails with the generic `invalid_or_oversized_data` and is omitted entirely from the ZIP; other position datasets succeed.

| November 14 export | History selection | Buy History | Sell History | Current positions | Manifest |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `PT-Data-2026-11-14T14-11-12Z.zip` | `7d` | Failed | Failed | 18 DCA; 0 pairs, pending, watchmode | `partial`; about 0.29 s |
| `PT-Data-GeorgeShort-2026-11-14T15-05-01Z.zip` (identical `(1)` re-upload) | `all` | Failed | Failed | 18 DCA; 0 pairs, pending, watchmode | `partial`; about 0.34 s |

The files contain `CurrentPositions/*.json` and `manifest.json`, **no** `Trades/buys.json` or `Trades/sales.json`; funding not selected. **No trade count of zero can be inferred** from failed datasets.

**Previous working baseline:** November 13 original **schema-1.0** ZIP exported **23 buys, 25 sales, 8 PAIRS and 2 DCA**, using the first raw PT response; it proves basic PT connectivity and record copying, **not** multi-page completeness. The major implementation change occurred in `618b6285d18e3c655139ae675bce389b72034ee5`, “replace record limits with paginated date-range trade exports.” It introduced `ReadHistoryAsync`, strict nested-JSON/page-size/record/duplicate/progress/timestamp/end validation and UTC range filtering. **It is the regression comparison boundary, not a proven failed condition.** No safe-specific-reason diagnostic change and no exporter fix were committed as of this handoff.

**Do not rule out the history-range change.** PT had been running for approximately three days, so `7d` and `all` cover the same available records and comparing them does not isolate a cause. The `all` path lacks date-filter timestamp checks, so both failing suggests a potentially shared validation/paging path, not a confirmed explanation. The manifest maps multiple `InvalidDataException` causes to the same error: unexpected `data` shape, page count greater than 25, malformed record, duplicate/conflicting IDs, repeated page/no progress, missing terminal page, size limits, and invalid timestamps where applicable. A ~0.3s failure suggests early rejection but **does not identify it**.

**Current code:** `Monitor/_Internal/PTDataExportService.cs`, `HistoryPageSize = 25`; 32 MiB per response, 128 MiB total source, 10,000-page cap, 120s export budget. Original trade fields and historical `BUYNOTE`/`SELLNOTE` `CV` diagnostics must remain intact.
