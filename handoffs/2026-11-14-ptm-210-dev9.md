## 2. Current metric contract and exclusions

**Active catalogue: 26 unique metrics**: 17 global-capable (including `PriceTrend`, which also supports asset scope) and **nine asset-only**, including `FundingRate`, `FundingSum`, `TakerBuyRatio`, `TakerImbalance`. Funding and taker flow are distinct data families. All MarketTrend scopes, eligible populations, timestamps, missing/stale semantics and explicit metric selection must be preserved.

**D-053 permanently removed** `OpenInterestChange`, `OpenInterestValueChange`, `GlobalLongShortAccountRatio` and all acquisition/active-cache integrations and proposed global aggregates dependent on those signals. Also previously removed `TopTraderLongShortAccountRatio` and `TopTraderLongShortPositionRatio` and optional API-key dependency. Do not restore as fallback, legacy compatibility or user-specific settings.

`TakerBuyRatio = Σ(taker-buy quote volume) / Σ(total quote volume)` over the exact latest complete UTC-aligned `MarketTrend.TrendMinutes` period; **range 0–1, dimensionless**. `TakerImbalance = 2 × TakerBuyRatio − 1`, **range −1 to 1**. These encode the **same** observation in different forms; not independent confirmation. Source: existing Binance kline index 10 (taker-buy **quote** volume, not base volume index 9). No extra endpoint is required. Missing, malformed, gapped, stale or zero-denominator history remains **UNAVAILABLE**, not fabricated zero. Eligible asset-scoped values only; do not create unrequested global variants.

Existing normalized OHLCV cache **v1 must remain readable**. New v2 persists nullable taker-buy quote volumes. Enabled taker trends may request targeted latest complete-period source repair, then persist upgraded coverage despite save throttle; normal OHLCV cold/warm-start functionality remains unchanged. Avoid repeatedly fetching full history if required taker horizon exceeds retention.
